Questions roofers ask before the call

FAQ

The short answers on qualification, attribution, payment, and the guarantee.

When does Roof Rhino get paid?+

Only when a tracked opportunity meets the agreed project and territory criteria, reaches the minimum value, has a signed agreement and confirmed payment pathway, clears the protected cancellation period, and passes duplicate review.

Is this pay per lead or pay per appointment?+

No. Leads and appointments are steps in the process. The product is a qualifying signed roofing customer that passes RoofLock™ verification.

What does Roof Rhino handle?+

Market underwriting, acquisition, qualification, calling and texting, scheduling, confirmations, reminders, no-show recovery, nurture, tracking, and verification.

What does the roofing partner handle?+

The physical inspection, final scope, final estimate, contract presentation, closing, accurate reporting, and professional fulfillment.

How does the First Roof Guarantee™ work?+

If the first Verified Won Roof is not produced within 45 days of the official Launch Date while all Partner Requirements are maintained, the partner may continue at no additional performance cost or request the return of unearned prepaid Roof Credits.

Can a billed roof be credited back?+

Yes. A covered duplicate, invalid qualification, protected-period cancellation, or other documented RoofLock™ failure is credited back under the agreed policy.

Do you work with every roofing company?+

No. Partners must have licensing, insurance, sales capacity, production capacity, appointment availability, reliable reporting, and a strong homeowner experience.
