Fast answers

What buyers usually ask next.

What exactly counts as a Verified Won Roof?+

A tracked opportunity that meets the agreed territory, project, and minimum-value criteria; has a signed roofing agreement and confirmed payment pathway; clears the protected cancellation period; and is not excluded by the duplicate policy.

Who owns advertising and follow-up?+

Roof Rhino owns acquisition, media management, speed-to-lead, qualification, scheduling, reminders, no-show recovery, and nurture. Your team owns the inspection, final scope, estimate, contract presentation, close, and fulfillment.

What happens when a homeowner is not ready?+

The opportunity returns to CloseLoop™ nurture. It does not create an acquisition fee unless it later meets every RoofLock™ condition.

Can guarantee coverage pause?+

Yes. If a partner repeatedly misses inspections, fails to follow up, withholds disposition data, or falls below the agreed operating standard, volume and guarantee coverage may pause until the issue is corrected.
