CLEAR TERMS

When the fee is earned.

A standard Verified Won Roof must meet every condition below.

  1. It originated through Roof Rhino's tracked acquisition system.
  2. It meets the agreed territory, project, and minimum-value criteria.
  3. The homeowner signed a roofing agreement.
  4. A payment or financing pathway is approved or confirmed.
  5. The applicable cancellation or rescission period has passed.
  6. It is not a pre-existing active opportunity under the duplicate policy.

A covered duplicate, invalid qualification, or protected-period cancellation is credited back.
